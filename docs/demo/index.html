Procedural line renderings

Two Web Components that draw architectural line drawings from a seed. Give them a box; they fill it. The seed comes from the clock unless you set one, and in bounce mode a new seed is drawn at the bottom of every cycle.

<dmg-street-corner>

A street corner seen through a pinhole camera: split-grammar facades with window families and rooftops, gaps in the street wall, ornament, and the street in front. Pitch tilts the camera up. Full is the crowded recipe with a back row, balconies, awnings and setbacks.

<dmg-massing>

An axonometric massing model: stepped terraces with railings, a few towers with floor lines, and a light study in three hatch weights. Hidden lines are removed by painting far to near.

Markup

<script src="procedural-line-renderings.js"></script>
